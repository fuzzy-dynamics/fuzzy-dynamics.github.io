# Introduction

At FYDY ("Fuzzy Dynamics, Inc.", "our", "we", "us"), we build AI systems for discovery and experimentation. We believe trust requires transparency about how we handle your information.

Our platform helps you explore ideas, evolve hypotheses, synthesize code from specifications, run computational experiments, and discover unexpected connections across your work. This page provides a high-level overview. For detailed legal documentation, data processing agreements, or compliance information, contact us at [team@fydy.ai](mailto:team@fydy.ai).

## What Data We Process

To deliver our AI-assisted research and experimentation capabilities, we process:

- **Your Content**: Hypotheses, experiments, code, notes, papers, and findings you create or upload
- **Interactions**: Conversations with AI assistants, prompts, and generated responses
- **Computational Work**: Code execution results, experimental parameters, and synthesis artifacts
- **Knowledge Organization**: How your work connects together, version history, and project structures
- **Integrations**: Content from services you connect (GitHub, Notion, Slack, etc.)
- **Team Collaboration**: Shared spaces and workflows for team accounts

## Core Principles

- **Privacy Mode**: Control how long your data is retained and whether it can be used for training
- **Encryption & Security**: Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Regional Storage**: Data stored on regional servers (US, India, EU) based on your location
- **Human Control**: You decide what gets processed, shared, or automated
- **Zero Retention Agreements**: Third-party AI providers cannot store or train on your data
- **No Ads, No Sales**: Your data is never used for advertising or sold to third parties

## Privacy Controls

**Privacy Mode** is available to all users (free and paid). It controls how long we retain your data and whether it can be used for training.

**When Privacy Mode is ON:**
- Data is processed to deliver our services but retained only as long as functionally necessary
- Automatically deleted when you remove projects or delete your account
- Never used to train models (ours or third-party)
- Zero data retention by AI providers

**When Privacy Mode is OFF:**
- Extended retention enables enhanced features like long-term memory and cross-session learning
- You can opt-in to allow your data to improve our models (requires explicit consent)
- All other privacy protections remain in place

**Your Controls:**
- Enable Privacy Mode globally or per-project
- Delete your account and all data at any time (complete removal within 30 days)
- Clear specific data types from your settings
- See what's being processed in real-time

## Security

**AI Processing**

**Intelligent Memory Systems**
