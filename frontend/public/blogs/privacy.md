# Introduction

Thank you for visiting our website! At FYDY ("Fuzzy Dynamics, Inc.", "our", "we", "us"), our sole concern is to make good new things for the world. We believe that truly good tools are built on trust—and trust requires transparency about how we handle the information you bring or create on Theatre. This isn't just about compliance; it's fundamental to building something worth using. 

For detailed terms and policies, please contact us at [team@fydy.ai](mailto:team@fydy.ai).

Theatre is an AI platform for discovery and engineering that helps you research, plan, build, review, and automate your workflow. To provide these capabilities, Theatre processes:

- **Content**: Source code, documentation, technical files, research data (notes, findings, experiments, and discovery artifacts) and workspace materials.
- **Context**: Structured connections between your data, ideas and implementations.
- **Collaboration Data**: Shared specs, reviews, and team communications.
- **Conversation Data**: Chat history, prompts, and AI-generated responses and artifacts.

As we continue developing Theatre, our policies may evolve—we'll keep you informed of any significant changes. Key principles include:

- **Privacy Mode**: When enabled, your content is never stored by us beyond the request lifecycle.
- **Human in the Loop**: Theatre is built around keeping humans in control. You decide what gets processed, shared, or automated.
- **Data Processing**: We process your content to power Theatre's features, but do not retain it.
- **Training by Opt-in**: We use your data for model training only when you explicitly opt-in.


# **Privacy Controls**

Privacy Mode: **Available to all users** (free and paid).
- When enabled, no code or research data is stored by us beyond request processing purposes.
- We have have zero data retention agreements with our third-party AI model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI).
- All knowledge graphs and embeddings are computed locally or deleted after use.

You are always in control: Always.

- Privacy Mode ON: No persistent storage of your content.
- Privacy Mode OFF: We may store prompts and interactions to improve Theatre.
- Granular permissions and explicit control over what Theatre can access.
- Real-time visibility into what data is being processed.

# **Security Approach**

Infrastructure: Most of our servers are in the US for US users, in India for Indian users and in the EU for EU users. This is to ensure the best latency and suitable data residency for our users.

- Hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure (AWS and GCP).
- SOC 2 compliance roadmap in progress.
- All requests are encrypted in transit.
- No data storage in alien regions without explicit consent.

AI & Third Parties: Theatre integrates with multiple AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI) to deliver its research and engineering capabilities. When you use Theatre:

- Requests may be routed to different AI providers based on the task.
- We maintain zero data retention agreements with all providers.

What we don't Do: Ever.

- Sell your data to third parties.
- Train models on your code without explicit consent.
- Share content between different user accounts or teams.
- Access your files outside of Theatre's explicit features.

Your Rights: You have the right to fetch, update, and delete your data.

- **Access**: Request, update and export (in standard formats) any data we have of you.
- **Deletion**: Complete account and data removal.
